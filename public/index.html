      🌍 How to Play Atlas

      Master the classic travel word-chain game with global multiplayer rules:

      1
      Chain the Letters: Each player must submit a geographical place that begins with the last letter of the previous place (e.g. Paris ➔ Singapore ➔ Egypt).
      2
      Broad Geography: Any country, major city, continent, ocean, sea, mountain range, island, or Indian state/river/lake is accepted!
      3
      No Repetitions: A place cannot be used more than once in the same round.
      4
      Speed = Maximum Points: You have 20 seconds. Answering within 5 seconds awards up to 200 points! Running out of time eliminates you from the round.
      5
      Tactical Lifelines: Use your 💡 Hint Token for clues or 🎲 Pass Ticket to avoid elimination on brutal letters!
